# after-merge

**D2 · Reviews and merges.** **Trigger:** something merged into dev.
**Packs:** fe-guides.

## Steps

1. `/book:progress` — task and feature status.
2. `/book:publish-guides` — if a guide changed.
3. `/book:check-sources` — only if the change rested on a source over a week old.

The status and the Drive copies match dev.
