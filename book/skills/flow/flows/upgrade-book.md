# upgrade-book

**G2 · Book upkeep.** **Trigger:** a new boilerplate version.
**Packs:** none.

## Steps

1. ✋ `/book:upgrade` — the report; each conflict and WRONG line with the person; then apply.
2. ⏸ `/book:self-review` — in a fresh session or a subagent; until READY.
3. `/book:commit`.
4. ✋ `/book:ship`.

## Loops

- ⟲ step 2 until READY, at most 3 rounds.

## Done when

Merged, and `book verify` is READY.
