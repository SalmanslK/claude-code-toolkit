# spec-says-x

**C2 · Defects and questions.** **Trigger:** QA or the FE report "the spec says X, the app does Y".
**Packs:** none.

## Steps

1. `/book:check-sources` — is our copy current?.
2. `/book:fact-check` — on that requirement.
3. `/book:raise` — `defect` (then `fix-defect`) or `question` (the FSD is unclear).
4. ✋ `/book:write-for` — the reporter; the person sends it.

## Done when

The reporter has an answer, and the Book holds the defect or the question.
