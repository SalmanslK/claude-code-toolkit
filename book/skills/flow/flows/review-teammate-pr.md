# review-teammate-pr

**D1 · Reviews and merges.** **Trigger:** a teammate's PR needs reviewing.
**Packs:** none.

## Steps

1. ⏸ `/book:peer-review` — in a fresh session; findings in the terminal.
2. ✋ The person approves posting; the comments are posted. Never approve or merge.
3. `/book:peer-review` — again, after the author's fixes.

## Loops

- ⟲ steps 2–3 until no blocking finding is open, at most 3 rounds.

## Done when

No blocking finding is open; the person approves in Bitbucket.
